FAQ

How do I grant microphone access?

Go to Settings → Decibel Meter → Microphone and toggle it on. The app needs microphone access to measure ambient sound levels.

Is the dB reading accurate?

Decibel Meter uses your device's built-in microphone for measurements. While it provides a reliable relative indication, professional-grade calibration equipment may yield more precise absolute values.

What is the Water Eject feature?

The 165 Hz frequency preset generates low-frequency vibrations through your speakers, which can help eject water droplets from the speaker grilles — similar to the Apple Watch water eject feature.

Does the app record my conversations?

No. The app processes audio in real-time on your device to calculate decibel levels. No audio data is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.

How can I remove ads?

Currently, the app is ad-supported and free. A future update may include an ad-free premium option.
