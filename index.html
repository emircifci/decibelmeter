Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Decibel Meter ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our iOS mobile application ("Application").

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Audio Recordings: The Application accesses your device's microphone exclusively to analyze sound amplitude (decibel calculation) and frequency on-device in real-time. We do not record, store, transcribe, or transmit your voice or any ambient audio data. All sound processing occurs entirely locally on your device.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or precise physical locations.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information to serve relevant ads in compliance with applicable laws and Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) guidelines.

Information collected by Google AdMob may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA if user consent is granted, or anonymous identifiers)
  • General approximate location (e.g., country/region)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)
  • Crash logs and app performance metrics

For more details on how Google uses data, please visit: Google Privacy & Terms

3. Microphone Permission

The Application requests access to the device microphone solely to measure environmental sound levels (dB SPL). You may revoke microphone access at any time through your iOS device settings: Settings > Decibel Meter > Microphone.

4. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
